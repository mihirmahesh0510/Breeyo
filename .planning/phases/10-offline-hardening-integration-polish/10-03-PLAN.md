| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Offline consultation drafts → replay service | Untrusted local clinical edits cross into trusted EMR persistence |
| Conflict-resolution UI → resolution actions | User-selected keep/merge/escalate actions can alter medical truth |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-05 | Tampering | Clinical replay envelopes | mitigate | Route all replay through `consultationOfflineReplay.service.ts` and shared schemas before touching consultation rows |
| T-10-06 | Information Disclosure | Offline consultation cache | mitigate | Limit offline EMR storage to active/today consultation context only per D-15 to D-17 |
| T-10-07 | Elevation of Privilege | Conflict-resolution actions | mitigate | Assign safety-critical ownership to the clinician via `recommendedOwnerUserId` and persist unresolved conflicts until explicit resolution |
</threat_model>
