<objective>
Build the anti-corruption layer that IS Phase 7's durable deliverable (WHA-04): a `WaProvider` port in domain vocabulary, a `SimulatorProvider` that enforces the real Cloud API's constraints rather than the simulator's freedoms, a `CloudApiProvider` skeleton, and a config-driven registry that selects between them.

Purpose: zero real Meta API calls happen in Beta, so the only lasting output of this phase is the shape of the boundary. If the port models what the simulator can do instead of what Meta allows, the swap becomes a rewrite. Making the simulator strict — template-only outside a 24-hour window, at most 3 quick-reply buttons of 20 characters, at most 10 list rows of 24 characters, pre-registered templates only, opaque provider message ids, asynchronous status transitions — is cheap now and expensive to retrofit. Every downstream plan calls through this port and never touches Meta shapes.

Output: the port, capability guards, the simulator adapter with its deterministic reply generator, a CloudApiProvider skeleton, the registry, a shared phone normalizer, and five unit suites.
</objective>
