- `export function placeAppointments(appointments: AppointmentWithDetails[], days: Date[], bounds: RowBounds): PlacedBlock[]` where `PlacedBlock` carries `{ appointment, dayIndex, rowIndex, rowSpan, columnIndex, columnCount, overflowCount }`. `rowSpan` is `Math.max(1, Math.ceil(durationMinutes / 30))` — the `Math.max(1, ...)` is what implements UI-SPEC's no-sub-row-sizing rule, because a proportionally sized 15-minute block would be 32px tall, below the 44px minimum target and too small for a pet name. Overlap resolution caps visible blocks at 3 per cell and reports the remainder as `overflowCount` on the last visible block.
