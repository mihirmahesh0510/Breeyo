<objective>
Build the Billing tab landing surface: the bottom-tab entry, the five summary cards (D-24's four plus D-33's RPT-01 patients-seen-today), the filterable and sortable invoice list, and the live-update subscription.

D-28 describes replacing a 'More' tab, but there is no 'More' tab in the shipped app — `apps/mobile/app/(app)/(tabs)/_layout.tsx` contains only `index` (Queue) and `patients`, and there is no Inventory tab because Phase 5's mobile work is separate. So D-28 reduces to appending a Billing tab; the "move More items to a drawer" clause has nothing to move. Record that in the summary rather than inventing a drawer.

The live-update path matters more here than it looks: a webhook lands on the server while the front desk is looking at the dashboard, and the Socket.IO `invoice:updated` event is what turns that into a visible status change. Without it the front desk polls by pulling to refresh, which is the behaviour D-09's payment flow explicitly avoids.

Purpose: RPT-01 and the read half of BIL-03.
Output: the tab, the dashboard screen, four components, three hooks, a shared money formatter, and two test files.
</objective>
