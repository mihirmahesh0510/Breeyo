- **Career status:** 7 years into her design career, stable and well-compensated. Willing to spend on premium pet care (food, grooming, vet visits) but annoyed by inefficiency in service interactions

### Pains
- **No access to pet medical records.** Ananya has no digital (or reliable paper) record of her pets' vaccination history, deworming schedule, past diagnoses, or prescriptions. When she visits a new vet or needs to board Simba, she cannot produce any documentation. She photographs paper prescriptions with her phone but loses track of them in her camera roll
- **Missed preventive care deadlines.** Vaccination boosters and deworming doses are due on specific schedules, but the clinic does not send reminders. Ananya relies on her own memory or phone calendar reminders, which she sets inconsistently. She has missed deworming doses by 2-3 weeks multiple times
- **Unpredictable wait times at the clinic.** Walk-in visits mean arriving and waiting with no visibility into queue position or estimated wait. With a sick or anxious animal, a 30-40 minute wait feels much longer. She has no way to check how busy the clinic is before leaving home
- **Payment friction at checkout.** The clinic provides handwritten bills and only accepts cash or informal Google Pay transfers to the vet's personal number. There is no formal invoice, no receipt for insurance claims (pet insurance is emerging in India), and no way to pay before arriving or after leaving
- **Communication with the clinic is ad hoc.** She WhatsApps the vet's personal number for post-visit questions ("Is this swelling normal?"), feels guilty about bothering the doctor, and sometimes does not get a reply for hours because the vet is in consultations

### What is This Person Trying to Accomplish?
- Access her pets' complete medical history (vaccinations, prescriptions, visit notes) from her phone at any time
- Receive automatic reminders when preventive care (vaccination, deworming) is due, via WhatsApp
- Know the estimated wait time at the clinic before she leaves home, or at least see her queue position after arriving
- Pay the clinic bill via UPI from a proper invoice with a payment link, and receive a digital receipt
- Communicate with the clinic through a channel that does not require bothering the vet's personal WhatsApp

### Goals
- **Short-term:** Stop missing Simba's deworming schedule; have a reliable way to know when the next dose is due without setting manual reminders
- **Long-term:** Have a single digital record of both pets' complete medical history that she owns and can share with any vet, boarder, or insurance provider
- **Personal:** Feel like a responsible pet parent who stays on top of her pets' health, not someone who "forgot the booster again"

### Attitudes & Influences
- **Decision-Making Authority:** No authority over which software the clinic uses. However, she influences the clinic indirectly: if Breeyo makes the clinic experience noticeably better (WhatsApp reminders, shorter perceived waits, digital records), she becomes a vocal advocate in her pet parent WhatsApp groups, driving word-of-mouth referrals for the clinic
- **Decision Influencers:** Pet parent WhatsApp group recommendations ("My vet sends me WhatsApp reminders for everything!"), Instagram pet community, her own professional UX sensibility (she notices and appreciates good digital experiences), and the vet's personal recommendation ("We use a new system now, you'll get your records on WhatsApp")
- **Beliefs & Attitudes:** Expects digital-first interactions in every service she uses (food delivery, banking, ride-hailing) and notices when vet clinics lag behind. Does not want to download another app -- WhatsApp is the right channel for clinic communication. Willing to pay more for a clinic that offers a modern experience but currently has no way to distinguish "digital" clinics from "paper" ones. Trusts her vet completely on medical decisions but wants transparency on billing and records

### Breeyo Touchpoints
- **Features used most:** Owner portal via magic link (Phase 9) -- this is her only direct interface with Breeyo. WhatsApp messages from the clinic (reminders, invoices, booking confirmations) (Phase 7). UPI payment via invoice link (Phase 6/9). She never logs into the clinic software and never will
- **Primary workflow:** Receives WhatsApp message from clinic ("Simba's Rabies booster is due in 7 days. Tap to book.") -> taps link -> confirms booking via WhatsApp flow -> visits clinic -> consultation happens -> receives invoice on WhatsApp with UPI payment link -> pays -> receives digital receipt. Between visits: opens magic link from WhatsApp to check vaccination history or upcoming due dates
- **What success looks like:** She never misses a vaccination or deworming dose because the clinic proactively reminds her. She has a link on WhatsApp she can open anytime to see Simba's complete medical history. She pays via UPI link and gets a proper invoice. She tells her pet parent WhatsApp group "My vet's clinic just sends everything on WhatsApp now, it's so convenient" -- and three other pet owners book appointments at the same clinic

### Assumptions to Validate [ASSUMPTION--VALIDATE]
- Pet owners will open and engage with WhatsApp messages from the clinic (reminders, invoices) rather than ignoring them as spam, especially if the clinic's WhatsApp number is not already saved in their contacts
- A magic-link-based owner portal (no login, no app download) is the right access model for pet owners, or they would prefer a lightweight app or a saved WhatsApp chat for record access
- Pet owners want to see their pet's medical records (diagnosis, prescriptions) digitally, or they trust the vet and do not feel the need to review clinical details
- UPI payment links sent via WhatsApp will be trusted and used by pet owners, or they will prefer paying in person (cash/Google Pay at the counter) due to trust and habit
- Vaccination/deworming reminders are the highest-value WhatsApp message for pet owners, or appointment confirmations and invoice receipts are more valued
- The pet owner persona is relevant for Breeyo's Beta launch (Phase 1-6), or owner-facing features only matter from Phase 7+ and this persona should not influence early product decisions
