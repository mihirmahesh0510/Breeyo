<objective>
Close the phase: prove every requirement with a named automated test, gate the invariants that no single plan owns, and put the flows a machine cannot judge in front of a human.

Three classes of failure survive per-plan verification. The first is a requirement that has tests in two plans and coverage in neither — a single gate script that names each requirement and runs its test makes that visible. The second is a phase-wide invariant such as "no float money anywhere" or "no route uses the admin client", which is asserted piecemeal in individual plans but never as a whole. The third is anything requiring judgement: whether a printed invoice looks like a document an Indian vet would hand to a client, whether a QR actually scans with a UPI app, and whether a real Razorpay webhook lands and flips the status without anyone touching the app.

Purpose: all eight requirements, verified rather than assumed.
Output: a phase gate script, a recorded verification document, and three human checkpoints.
</objective>

  <what-built>
Two things in this phase are legally or operationally load-bearing and neither can be signed off by a test.

**GST correctness.** The engine implements per-line `taxTreatment`, so veterinary healthcare services are treated as exempt under Notification 12/2017-CT(R) Entry 46 (SAC 998351) while pet food, accessories and dispensed medicines are taxable. Slabs are `0 / 5 / 18 / 40` per GST 2.0 effective 22 September 2025 — the retired 12% and 28% slabs are deliberately absent. Rounding is per tax head at invoice level under Section 170 and Rule 51, with a persisted round-off delta. Document typing follows CGST Rule 46A. GST defaults **off** per clinic because most solo vets are below the ₹20 lakh registration threshold, and collecting tax without registration is a Section 122 offence.

**Razorpay onboarding.** D-29 locks per-clinic API keys, which means each of the 20 pilot clinics needs its own Razorpay account with completed KYC and its own webhook configured in their own dashboard with the per-clinic URL shown in Billing Settings. That is external, third-party-paced work that the build cannot unblock, and a clinic that skips the webhook step has automatic payment confirmation silently broken.
  </what-built>
