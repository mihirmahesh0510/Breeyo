<objective>
Assemble the invoice builder screen from the state, hooks and components plan 06-16 built, and wire the two routes that reach it.

Split from plan 06-16 for context budget: that plan owns the store, the mutation and catalog hooks, and the eight presentation components; this plan owns the screen that composes them and the two Expo Router entry points. The seam is clean because 06-16's deliverables are all independently unit-tested, so this plan's job is composition, the debounced preview wiring, and the two 409 paths.

The controlling constraint carries over unchanged: the client never computes money. The builder shows a live total while the front desk edits, but that total comes from the server's `preview-totals` endpoint, and `finalize` sends only the line items and options — never a total. A client that computed and sent `grandTotal` could be modified to send `1`.

The second constraint is that BIL-02's stock validation is a server 409, not a client check. The draft may have been assembled minutes earlier, so the builder must handle the 409 gracefully: render the shortfall, keep the draft editable, and unblock finalize when the offending quantity changes.

Purpose: BIL-01's interactive path, BIL-02's client surface, and BIL-07's builder-side presentation.
Output: two routes, one screen, and one screen-state test file.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| builder form → finalize request | Everything the device sends is untrusted; the invoice's money must not be among it |
| server preview response → displayed total | The number the front desk reads before collecting payment |
| 409 error payload → stock banner | The client renders a claim about inventory it cannot itself verify |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-102 | Tampering | a modified client sending `grandTotalPaise: 1` on finalize | mitigate | No total field exists in the store (plan 06-16) or in any request body this screen builds; the input schemas from plan 06-04 do not accept totals; a screen test asserts the finalize body has no total-shaped key; the server recomputes regardless |
| T-06-106 | Repudiation | finalizing against stale stock and overselling | accept | The client cannot prevent this; the server's row-locked finalize transaction is the control. The client's role is to render the resulting 409 clearly and keep the draft editable — the banner and the disabled-until-changed finalize button |
| T-06-107 | Information Disclosure | a previous patient's line items leaking into a new invoice | mitigate | Store `reset()` in the unmount cleanup; a test asserts an empty store on remount |
| T-06-108 | Tampering | silently losing the user's edits when the invoice was finalized elsewhere | mitigate | `INVOICE_NOT_DRAFT` surfaces an explicit message and navigates to the detail view rather than retrying or discarding |
| T-06-139 | Tampering | a stale preview total left on screen after a failed refresh, shown to the person about to collect payment | mitigate | The last successful breakdown renders dimmed during a refresh rather than blanking or being replaced by client arithmetic; `InvoiceTotalsSection` (plan 06-16) performs no arithmetic and takes a server-computed `TaxBreakdown` |
</threat_model>
