    - "Generate Certificate" button (outlined, primary) calls vaccination certificate PDF generation
    - On consultation finalize: creates VaccinationRecord via POST /api/v1/pets/:petId/vaccinations

    - Similar to VaccinationForm but simpler:

    - Text: "[Pet Name] has a consultation in progress. Tap to resume."
