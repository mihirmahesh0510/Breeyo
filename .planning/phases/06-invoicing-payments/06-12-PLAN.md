<objective>
Complete the billing API: the dashboard aggregate that RPT-01 and D-24 both feed from, the D-02 service catalog CRUD the invoice builder consumes, and the D-29 billing settings that hold the per-clinic Razorpay credentials.

RPT-01 was pulled into Phase 6 during a coverage-gap analysis and is defined by ROADMAP success criterion #5. D-33 resolves it as a fifth summary card alongside D-24's four, computed as a distinct-pet count over today's finalized consultations. The timezone matters: UTC midnight is 05:30 IST, so a UTC-based "today" silently drops every morning consultation from the count. The repository already has `QueueRepository.getTodayIST()` and it must be reused rather than reinvented.

The settings endpoint is the highest-sensitivity read surface in the phase. `06-UI-SPEC.md` puts a "Razorpay Key Secret" input in Billing Settings, which means a live payment credential is transmitted to and stored by the API. It must be encrypted at rest and must never be returned in any response, not even masked-but-recoverable.

Purpose: RPT-01, the catalog half of BIL-01, the settings half of BIL-05, and the clinic-configuration half of BIL-07.
Output: three services, three controllers, ten routes, and three integration test files.
</objective>
