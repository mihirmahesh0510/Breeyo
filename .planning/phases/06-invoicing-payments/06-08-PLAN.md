<objective>
Expose the invoice domain over HTTP with correct authorization, and prove BIL-01, BIL-02, BIL-03 and the invoice half of BIL-07 with database-backed integration tests.

Billing is the first consumer of `requirePermission` — the middleware exists at `apps/api/src/middleware/authorize.ts:13-39` and is currently unused outside auth. It is also where D-05 gets enforced: `apps/api/prisma/seed.ts:52` grants `CREATE_INVOICES` to the Clinician role, which contradicts D-05's "Clinicians cannot create invoices directly." The subtlety is that removing it must not break D-03, because the End-Consultation draft-creation path is triggered *by* a Clinician. Those are two distinct authorization surfaces and must be planned as such: the interactive endpoint is gated, the internal service call is not.

Purpose: BIL-01, BIL-02, BIL-03. This is also where the tenant-client pattern established in plan 06-02 gets applied to a new module for the first time.
Output: schema, controller, routes, app registration, the D-05 seed correction, and three integration test files.
</objective>
