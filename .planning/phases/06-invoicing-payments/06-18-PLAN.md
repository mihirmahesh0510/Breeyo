<objective>
Complete the two remaining read-and-sell surfaces: the Quick Sale counter-sale screen and the pet-profile Invoices section.

Quick Sale is the POS path — scan or search, cart, one tap. Its cart deliberately diverges from the invoice builder's store: adding the same product twice merges into one line with quantity two, because a counter sale of two identical tins is one line, whereas two consultations are two billable events. Its totals, like the builder's, come from the server's preview endpoint, because a client-computed counter-sale total that disagrees with the invoice is an argument at the counter with a customer holding cash.

The pet-profile Invoices section is the smaller half but has one non-obvious requirement: the change to `PatientDetailScreen.tsx` must be purely additive. That file is a Phase 3 deliverable with its own passing test suite, and restructuring it to add a section is how a billing feature breaks patient management.

Scope note: this plan was split for context budget. The billing settings screen — the phase's most sensitive client surface, with its write-only Razorpay credential fields and its GST guard rails — is plan 06-23, which runs in an earlier wave and shares no files with this plan.

Purpose: BIL-01's counter-sale path, BIL-02's Quick Sale surface, and BIL-07's per-clinic GST display in the cart totals.
Output: one route, one screen, three components, one hook module, one store, an addition to the pet profile, and two test files.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-122 | Tampering | a client-computed counter-sale total disagreeing with the invoice, creating a dispute at the counter | mitigate | `QuickSaleTotals` performs no arithmetic and renders a server preview; the cart store holds no total; grep gates assert both |

| T-06-123 | Tampering | a Quick Sale overselling stock | accept | The client cannot prevent this; the server's row-locked transaction is the control. The client renders the resulting per-row shortfall and disables checkout until it is resolved |
| T-06-124 | Information Disclosure | a previous customer's cart persisting into the next sale | mitigate | Store `reset()` on unmount; no Zustand persistence middleware is used |
</threat_model>
