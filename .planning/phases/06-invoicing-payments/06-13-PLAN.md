<objective>
Wire the two remaining invoice-creation paths from D-03 and D-04.

D-03's End-Consultation hook is the primary way invoices come into existence, and it has a specific authorization shape: the trigger is a Clinician, and D-05 removes `CREATE_INVOICES` from the Clinician role, so this path must be a server-initiated internal service call with no permission check — not an HTTP request the client makes on the vet's behalf. It must also never block the clinical finalize: a billing failure cannot prevent a vet from closing a consultation, so the call is best-effort with the failure surfaced through the logger, following the precedent already in `emr.service.ts` for the dosage-override audit.

D-04's Quick Sale is the POS path: scan or search, cart, one tap to create and finalize an invoice with no consultation. It reuses the same stock-locking transaction as a consultation invoice — a counter sale that oversells is the same bug as a consultation invoice that oversells.

Purpose: BIL-01's second and third creation paths, and BIL-02 coverage for the counter-sale path.
Output: the EMR hook, the Quick Sale service, controller and route plugin, app wiring, and two integration test files.
</objective>
