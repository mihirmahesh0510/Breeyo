<objective>
Create the Phase 6 persistence layer: ten billing tables, the D-29 clinic billing settings, their migration, and their RLS policies.

Every money column is `Int` paise per D-31, matching the already-shipped `ServiceCatalog.price: Int`. Phase 5's `InventoryItem.sellingPrice` stays `Decimal(10,2)` rupees and is converted at the line-item boundary by the `toPaise()` adapter built in plan 06-05 — no Phase 5 table is modified here. Every rate, price and tax amount that a finalized invoice depends on is frozen onto the invoice row so a historical invoice can be re-derived and defended in a GST audit without reading current settings.

Purpose: BIL-01 through BIL-07 and RPT-01 all read and write these tables. The partial unique index on draft-per-consultation is what makes the D-03 "End Consultation creates a draft" path idempotent under retry; the unique index on `webhook_events.event_id` is what makes BIL-06 idempotent under Razorpay's documented duplicate delivery.
Output: schema, migration, RLS policies, test factories, and a database-shape test.
</objective>
