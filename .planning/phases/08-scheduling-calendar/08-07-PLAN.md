<objective>
Build the appointment half of the scheduling module: the lifecycle guard, the repository (including every query plan 08-09's sweep will need), and the service that creates, reschedules, cancels, checks in and completes appointments.

Purpose: this is SCH-01. D-02 (per-service duration, snapshotted), D-07 (90-day horizon), D-14 (double-book with a warning, never a hard block), D-19 (owner/pet selection by the same lookup as check-in), D-20 (the five-state lifecycle), D-21 (one appointment, many pets) and D-22 (bounded recurrence) all land here. RESEARCH Pitfall 6 is the subtle one: mutating `scheduledFor` in place on a reschedule strands reminder tasks and sweep markers keyed on the appointment id, so the reschedule path must explicitly reset them.
Deliberately NOT built here, per D-03: no walk-in-capacity reservation and no configurable buffer or reserved-capacity setting. An appointment claims its exact slot and nothing more; walk-ins fill in around it through Phase 3's existing queue-position logic as extended by plan 08-04. Any "reserve N slots for walk-ins" affordance would contradict a locked decision.
Output: `appointment.state.ts`, `appointment.repository.ts`, `appointment.service.ts`, and unit tests for the transition table and every service rule.
</objective>
