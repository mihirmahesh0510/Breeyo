<objective>
Build the invoice builder: the screen where a draft becomes a finalized, numbered, GST-correct invoice.

The controlling constraint is that the client never computes money. The builder shows a live total while the front desk edits, but that total comes from the server's `preview-totals` endpoint, and `finalize` sends only the line items and options — never a total. A client that computed and sent `grandTotal` could be modified to send `1`. The server recomputes from persisted line items on finalize and ignores anything else; the builder's job is to render whatever it returns.

The second constraint is that BIL-02's stock validation is a server 409, not a client check. A read-only pre-check for the banner is a UX affordance; the authoritative validation holds row locks inside the finalize transaction, and the builder must handle the 409 gracefully because the draft may have been assembled minutes earlier.

Purpose: BIL-01's interactive path, BIL-02's client surface, and BIL-07's builder-side presentation.
Output: two routes, one screen, eight components, two hook modules, one store, and two test files.
</objective>
