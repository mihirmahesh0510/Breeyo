<objective>
Build the event side of the system: the single delivery-status funnel with monotonic ranking and an append-only ledger, the inbound router that turns normalized events into domain actions, the outbound and simulator workers, and the unauthenticated webhook endpoint with raw-body HMAC verification.

Purpose: WHA-05 requires that every message flow be logged, and Meta guarantees neither status ordering nor at-most-once webhook delivery. One funnel plus rank comparison plus an append-only event table is what makes the `Queued → Sent → Delivered` ladder truthful under out-of-order and duplicated events. Building the webhook now — even though the simulator never calls it — is the concrete proof that WHA-04's swap is configuration rather than code, and it is where the phase's highest-severity control (signature verification before parsing) actually runs.

Output: delivery-status service, inbound router with injectable booking and reminder handlers, two workers with test-guarded construction and directly callable handlers, the webhook plugin, four unit suites, and the status-ledger and webhook-idempotency integration suites.
</objective>
