### Pains
- **Administrative burden steals clinical time.** She spends 1.5-2 hours daily on paper registers, manual billing, and stock checks -- roughly 30% of her working hours. This time directly reduces the number of patients she can see
- **No retrievable medical history.** Pet records are in paper files organized by owner surname. Finding a returning patient's vaccination history or past prescriptions takes 3-5 minutes of flipping through registers. When a file goes missing, the history is gone
- **Inventory blind spots cause consultation delays.** She discovers a drug is out of stock mid-consultation and has to send her assistant to the nearby pharmacy, making the pet owner wait 15-20 minutes. Expired stock is caught only during periodic manual checks
- **Billing is error-prone and non-compliant.** Invoices are handwritten or generated in a basic Excel template. GST filing requires her CA to manually re-enter every transaction. She has been warned about non-compliance but fixing it feels overwhelming
- **No way to send automated reminders.** She knows follow-up reminders and vaccination due-dates would bring pet owners back, but she has no system to track or send them. She occasionally sends manual WhatsApp messages but forgets more often than not

### What is This Person Trying to Accomplish?
- See 20-25 patients per day without staying past 8 PM to finish paperwork
- Find any returning patient's records (vaccination history, past prescriptions, owner contact) in under 10 seconds
- Generate a GST-compliant invoice at the end of each consultation without manual calculation
- Keep track of drug stock levels so she never runs out of critical items mid-consultation
- Send vaccination and deworming reminders to pet owners through WhatsApp without doing it manually

### Goals
- **Short-term:** Eliminate paper registers and move to digital records within 1 month of adopting Breeyo, without disrupting daily patient flow
- **Long-term:** Run a fully digital clinic where medical records, billing, inventory, and pet owner communication are handled in one system she can access from her phone
- **Personal:** Reclaim evenings for family by finishing all admin work during clinic hours, not after
